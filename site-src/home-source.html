Research

I develop robust machine learning methods for large-scale spatiotemporal data, studying how self-supervised and generative models learn hierarchical, multi-scale structure, including a single embedding whose nested subspaces capture coarse-to-fine clusters (paper). My work includes developing machine learning methods for robust generalization under distribution shift, enabling effective few-shot and zero-shot adaptation for online sequence-to-text decoding (paper), developing multimodal foundation models + language models, with a focus on scalable training, efficient on-device optimization, and robust real-time decoding under real-world kinematic variation for the Meta Neural Band.
